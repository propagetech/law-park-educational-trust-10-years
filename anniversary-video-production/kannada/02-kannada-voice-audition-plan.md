# 02 · ಕನ್ನಡ ಧ್ವನಿ ಆಯ್ಕೆ ಯೋಜನೆ · Kannada Voice Audition Plan

For the Kannada event master of the Law Park Educational Trust ten-year welcome film.

---

## 0 · What this document can and cannot do

**I have not auditioned any voice.** This session has no access to a text-to-speech platform, no audio device and no Kannada voice library, so no scores below are filled in and no recording has been made. Filling in a scorecard I did not hear would be worse than useless on a film whose whole register depends on how one voice sounds in a hall.

What follows is the complete audition instrument: the copy, the protocol, the scorecard, the shortlist to test, and a decision rule. Someone with platform access runs it in about ninety minutes and the result is auditable.

**One recommendation before any of it: use a human Kannada narrator.** See section 1.

---

## 1 · The primary recommendation: a human voice

For a five-minute film that will be projected to a hall containing the children it is about, their parents, rural school staff, trustees and Kannada press, a **live-recorded native Kannada narrator is the right choice**, and synthetic speech is a fallback rather than a peer.

Three specific reasons, not general ones:

1. **The film is built on pauses.** There are 17 seconds of marked pause inside the narration and 24 seconds of deliberate silence around it. The two-second hold after `ಪೂರ್ತಿ ಅಲ್ಲ.` and the 2.5-second hold after `ಮುನ್ನೂರು.` are the emotional architecture of the whole cut. Synthetic voices place breath and decay mechanically; a narrator who understands why the pause is there will shape the syllable *before* it, which is the part that actually lands.
2. **Seven lines have alternate readings pending trustee review** (`01` section 6). A human narrator reads all three variants of `ಬಂದು ನಿಂತಿದೆ` aloud in one take and the trustees choose by ear. That is exactly what `14` item 7.1 requires, and it is a conversation, not a render.
3. **Cost and provenance.** `05` section 6 already makes this point about music: commissioning a Karnataka artist is cheaper than premium library licensing and is a better story. The same is true of the voice. A Bengaluru voice artist for a five-minute NGO film is a modest, one-session cost, and the release (`14` item 5.5) is straightforward.

Where to look: Kannada theatre and radio voice artists in Bengaluru, Akashvani Bengaluru freelancers, Kannada audiobook and documentary narrators, and Kannada-medium school teachers with stage experience. **Audition them with the same copy and the same scorecard below.** The instrument is identical whether the voice is human or synthetic.

## 2 · Voice profile · primary

Carried from the brief and confirmed against the script's actual demands.

| Attribute | Specification |
|---|---|
| Gender | **Female** |
| Age impression | 35 to 50 |
| Tone | Warm, composed, dignified, grounded |
| Pace | **105 words per minute** (band 100 to 110). This is the design pace of the timeline in `01` |
| Accent | Neutral Karnataka Kannada, Bengaluru-friendly standard register |
| Register | A respected Kannada-medium headmistress addressing a hall. Not a stage orator, not a vlogger, not a newsreader |
| Energy map | Medium-low at `K02`, steady through `K14` to `K23`, quiet and flat at `K27`, warm from `K38`, gently open at `K45` |
| Must be able to | hold a two-second silence without dropping intention; pronounce ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿವೇತನ six times without compressing it; say English proper nouns (Law Park Educational Trust, Bharat Shiksha Ratan) cleanly inside a Kannada sentence |
| Must avoid | high pitch, youthful brightness, theatrical swell, radio-jockey lilt, sales warmth, devotional cadence, trailer gravity |

## 3 · Voice profile · alternative

| Attribute | Specification |
|---|---|
| Gender | Male |
| Age impression | 40 to 55 |
| Tone | Resonant, gentle baritone, confident but humble |
| Pace | 95 to 105 wpm. **Note:** at 95 wpm the timeline in `01` lengthens by roughly 26 seconds to about 05:30. Re-derive the timeline; do not compress the pauses to absorb it |
| Delivery | A respected school headmaster speaking to a community gathering |
| Must avoid | aggressive baritone, newsreader stiffness, political-platform delivery, deep trailer voice |

## 4 · Audition copy

### 4.1 Test A · the brief's excerpt, exactly as specified

Record this verbatim. 25 to 35 seconds.

> ಶಿಕ್ಷಣವು ಒಂದು ಮಗುವಿನ ಹಕ್ಕು ಮಾತ್ರವಲ್ಲ.
> ಅದು ಒಂದು ಕುಟುಂಬದ ನಾಳೆಯೂ ಹೌದು.
> 2016ರಲ್ಲಿ, ಚಿಕ್ಕಬಳ್ಳಾಪುರದ ಒಂದು ಶಾಲಾ ಭೇಟಿಯಿಂದ
> ಲಾ ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಎಜುಕೇಷನಲ್ ಟ್ರಸ್ಟ್‌ನ ಪಯಣ ಆರಂಭವಾಯಿತು.
> ಒಬ್ಬ ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗೆ ಸ್ಕಾಲರ್‌ಶಿಪ್.
> ಒಂದು ಸಣ್ಣ ಆರಂಭ.
> ಆದರೆ, ಮುಂದುವರಿದ ಒಂದು ಭರವಸೆ.

**Direction**
- Calm pace throughout, never rushed.
- Pause about **1 second** after ಹಕ್ಕು ಮಾತ್ರವಲ್ಲ.
- Pause **1.5 to 2 seconds** after ಒಬ್ಬ ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗೆ ಸ್ಕಾಲರ್‌ಶಿಪ್.
- Gentle emphasis on **2016ರಲ್ಲಿ**, **ಒಬ್ಬ ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗೆ ಸ್ಕಾಲರ್‌ಶಿಪ್**, **ಮುಂದುವರಿದ ಒಂದು ಭರವಸೆ**.
- Do not over-emphasise sadness.
- End on calm conviction, not a dramatic rise.

> **One flag on this copy, for the record.** It contains `ಸ್ಕಾಲರ್‌ಶಿಪ್`, and the production glossary (`07` section 7, `12` section 12) fixes **ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿವೇತನ** for the whole film, sourced from Udayavani's own coverage of LPET and independently confirmed on an LPET banner in asset `A230`. The word ಸ್ಕಾಲರ್‌ಶಿಪ್ is never spoken in the finished film. Test A is still recorded exactly as written, because it is the specified instrument and because a synthetic voice's handling of an English loanword inside a Kannada sentence is itself worth hearing. But the deciding test is 4.2.

### 4.2 Test B · the deciding excerpt, from the actual film

Test A is comfortable copy. Test B is the copy the film really asks for: a six-syllable term repeated, three one-word sentences, a two-word turn, and two held silences. **A voice that passes A and fails B is the wrong voice.** 40 to 45 seconds.

> 2016ರಲ್ಲಿ ಅವರು ಮೊದಲ ಶಾಲಾ ಭೇಟಿ ಮಾಡಿದರು.
> ಚಿಕ್ಕಬಳ್ಳಾಪುರ. ಒಂದು ಮಗು. ಒಂದು ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿವೇತನ.
> *(1 ಸೆಕೆಂಡು ಹಿಡಿಯಿರಿ)*
> ಅದೇ ಭೇಟಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ನೂರೈವತ್ತು ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗಳಿಗೆ ಸ್ಟೀಲ್ ತಟ್ಟೆ ಮತ್ತು ಲೋಟ ವಿತರಿಸಲಾಯಿತು.
> *(2 ಸೆಕೆಂಡು ಹಿಡಿಯಿರಿ)*
> ನಂತರ ಟ್ರಸ್ಟ್ ಶಾಲಾ ಶುಲ್ಕದ ಶೇಕಡ ಎಪ್ಪತ್ತೈದರಷ್ಟನ್ನು ಭರಿಸುತ್ತದೆ. ಪೂರ್ತಿ ಅಲ್ಲ.
> *(2 ಸೆಕೆಂಡು ಹಿಡಿಯಿರಿ)*
> ಮತ್ತು ಆ ಹಣ ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಶಾಲೆಗೇ ಸಲ್ಲುತ್ತದೆ. ಬೇರೆ ಯಾರ ಕೈಗೂ ಅಲ್ಲ.

**What Test B is listening for**
- `ಚಿಕ್ಕಬಳ್ಳಾಪುರ. ಒಂದು ಮಗು. ಒಂದು ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿವೇತನ.` Three sentences, three full stops, no rising list intonation. Most synthetic voices read this as a comma list. That is an automatic fail.
- `ಪೂರ್ತಿ ಅಲ್ಲ.` Two words at the same volume as the sentence before, then stop. Any emphasis reads as defensiveness.
- `ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿವೇತನ` and `ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿಗಳಿಗೆ` in adjacent sentences, both intact, neither compressed.
- `ಎಪ್ಪತ್ತೈದರಷ್ಟನ್ನು` as one word, not four syllables and a stumble.
- Held silences that stay alive rather than sounding like the file ended.

### 4.3 Test C · the closing line

Two takes, no direction beyond "warm, and then stop".

> ಲಾ ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಎಜುಕೇಷನಲ್ ಟ್ರಸ್ಟ್‌ನ ಹತ್ತನೇ ವರ್ಷದ ಸಂಭ್ರಮಕ್ಕೆ ನಿಮಗೆಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಆತ್ಮೀಯ ಸ್ವಾಗತ.

This is the line the hall hears last. Listen for the conjunct in **ಸಂಭ್ರಮ** and for whether the final **ಸ್ವಾಗತ** stays level. A lift on the last syllable makes it an advertisement.

## 5 · Shortlist to test

**Verify every identifier below against the platform's current voice list before you rely on it.** Voice catalogues change, names are retired, and nothing here has been checked from this session. Search each platform for voices explicitly labelled **Kannada** or **Kannada (India) / kn-IN** and ignore anything offered as "Indian English" or as a nearby language.

| # | Where to look | What to search for | Expect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Human, Bengaluru** | Kannada documentary and audiobook narrators; Akashvani Bengaluru freelancers; Kannada theatre voice artists | The recommended route. Three candidates, same copy, same scorecard |
| 2 | **Google Cloud Text-to-Speech** | locale `kn-IN`; the Standard and higher-quality Kannada voice tiers, female and male | Long-standing native Kannada support. Test at least one female and one male |
| 3 | **Microsoft Azure AI Speech** | locale `kn-IN`; the neural Kannada voices, female and male | Native Kannada neural voices; the female voice is the one to test against the primary profile |
| 4 | **Indian TTS providers** | Sarvam AI, AI4Bharat / IndicTTS, Reverie, and comparable India-focused vendors; filter to Kannada | Often the best prosody on Indian proper nouns and on Kannada conjuncts, because Indic languages are the product rather than an add-on |

**Do not** use a multilingual model that merely tolerates Kannada text, and do not use an English voice reading Kannada transliterated into Roman letters. Both produce the flat, mispronounced Kannada that `09` section 4 explicitly warns against, and a Kannada-speaking hall hears it immediately.

**Shortlist at least three candidates.** If fewer than three genuinely native Kannada voices are available on the chosen platform, that is itself the finding, and it is the argument for section 1.

## 6 · Scorecard

Score each candidate 1 to 5. Run Test A, then Test B, then Test C. **Listen on a large speaker or a PA, not on headphones**, because the deliverable plays in a hall.

| Criterion | Weight | What a 5 sounds like | What a 1 sounds like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kannada pronunciation | x2 | Conjuncts and vowel signs intact: ಸಂಭ್ರಮ, ಗ್ರಂಥಾಲಯ, ಮುಖ್ಯೋಪಾಧ್ಯಾಯರು | Conjuncts broken into separate syllables |
| Clarity on a large system | x2 | Every word intelligible at 25 m from a 4 m screen | Sibilance and mud; consonants lost |
| Warmth | x1 | Present, unforced | Either cold or sugary |
| Humility and restraint | x2 | States facts; never sells | Performs sympathy |
| Proper nouns and numbers | x2 | ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿವೇತನ, ನೂರೈವತ್ತು, ಎಪ್ಪತ್ತೈದರಷ್ಟನ್ನು, Law Park Educational Trust | Compresses, guesses, or anglicises |
| Natural pauses | x2 | Holds 2 s alive; shapes the syllable before the silence | Pauses read as dropouts or are ignored |
| Freedom from synthetic artefact | x1 | No metallic tail, no clipped word ends, no wobble on long vowels | Audible seams, especially on Kannada geminates |
| Three-sentence test (`ಚಿಕ್ಕಬಳ್ಳಾಪುರ. ಒಂದು ಮಗು. ಒಂದು ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿವೇತನ.`) | **x3** | Three sentences, three stops, no lift | One rising list |
| `ಪೂರ್ತಿ ಅಲ್ಲ.` | **x3** | Level, quiet, final | Emphasised, apologetic, or swallowed |

## 7 · Decision rule

1. Score all candidates on A, B and C.
2. **Primary** is the highest weighted total that also scores 4 or 5 on both x3 criteria.
3. **Fallback** is the next highest from a *different* source, so a platform outage or a licence problem does not stop the production.
4. Present the primary and the fallback to the trustees **as the 40-second Test B sample, not as a description**, together with the measured words per minute.
5. **Do not record the full script until the trustees approve the sample.** This is the brief's instruction and it is also practical: the seven pending line variants in `01` section 6 should be settled in the same conversation.
6. Record Version A only. If the online cut later wants the intimate Version B register from `09`, book a separate pass; `14` item 7.7 requires the two registers to be performed separately rather than one read twice.

**Recommendation to carry into that meeting:** a human Kannada narrator matching the section 2 profile, with the best-scoring native `kn-IN` synthetic voice held as the fallback. If the schedule forces synthetic for the event master, record the human version afterwards for the YouTube and social cuts; the visual master does not change, only the narration stem (`10` section F).

## 8 · Recording direction for the booked session

| | |
|---|---|
| Format | WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono |
| Room | Treated booth or a quiet room with soft furnishing. No reverb; the hall adds its own |
| Mic | Large-diaphragm condenser at 20 to 25 cm, slightly off-axis, pop filter |
| Processing at record | None beyond a gentle high-pass. **No compression, no de-esser, no limiter on the recorded file** |
| Processing at mix | Gentle compression and gentle de-essing only. **Preserve the Kannada consonants**: aggressive de-essing destroys ಶ, ಷ, ಸ and the retroflex ಳ, and those distinctions carry meaning |
| Peaks | never above -3 dBTP |
| Loudness | event master -23 LUFS, online master -16 LUFS |
| Takes | three of every line minimum; **five of `K45`**, the closing welcome |
| Years | record every year both as Kannada number words and as English digits (`09` section 3.4) and choose in the edit |
| Room tone | 30 seconds, for the silent cards and for edit repairs |
| Order of recording | Read the film in order, once, without stopping, before any pickup work. The energy map in section 2 only makes sense as a single pass |
| Present in the session | the director, a native Kannada speaker, and if at all possible a trustee, so the seven pending variants can be decided by ear on the spot |

**Brief the narrator on one thing above all else.** This is a welcome, not an appeal. Nobody in the film is being rescued. The people in the hall are the people in the photographs, and many of them paid their own quarter of the fee every year for ten years. Read it to them, not about them.
