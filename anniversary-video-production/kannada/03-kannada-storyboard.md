## How to read this document

- **Asset ID** resolves in [`04-media-inventory.csv`](../../anniversary-video-research/04-media-inventory.csv).
- **Every path was checked against the filesystem.** Three paths given in earlier documents do not exist as written; the corrections are in [`06`](06-kannada-edit-decision-list.md) section 2.
- **Consent status is my own frame-by-frame assessment**, made by opening each image, not copied from the inventory. Where the two disagree, both are shown and the disagreement is flagged. There are four such corrections and all four make the inventory's position *less* safe than it claims.
- **`[C] BLOCKING`** means the shot shows identifiable minors and cannot reach picture lock without a signed guardian release. See [`07`](07-final-rights-and-approval-checklist.md).
- **Crops are given as pixel windows on the native file.** Every upscale factor is stated. Nothing is upscaled beyond 2.2x, and everything above 1.6x is flagged for a projector check.
- **Always grade from the `.jpg` or `.jpeg`, never the `.webp`.** `05` section 2.2. One asset, `A082`, exists only as WebP; that is flagged where it occurs.

## The six things this cut does that the English cut does not

Carried from [`10`](../../anniversary-video-research/10-bilingual-storyboard-and-edit-plan.md) section C and extended by what the images actually turned out to contain.

| # | Difference | Where |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Opens on a Kannada government school board, held 7 seconds in silence.** In the English cut the board is texture. Here it is a sentence the audience reads, and it names H.D. Kote without a caption | `K01` |
| 2 | **Every title and statistics card gets 1.3x the English dwell.** Budgeted into the durations, not added at the end | all 9 cards |
| 3 | **The founder's quote card runs 6 seconds with no narration, no music and no photograph.** It is the longest silence in the film | `K06` |
| 4 | **The 2020 relief poster gets a Kannada gloss strip.** The poster itself is in English; without the gloss the beat excludes the audience it is addressed to | `K17` |
| 5 | **The school board returns at a tighter focal length in act three, held 1 second longer.** A documentary rhyme, not a repeat | `K25` |
| 6 | **The Udayavani clipping holds 2 seconds longer and pushes into the Kannada caption.** In English it proves coverage exists. In Kannada it is a document the audience reads for itself. This is the one moment where the Kannada film is simply better than the English one | `K36` |
