This is a reference cut, not the final render

The video below exists so that pacing, shot order, on-screen Kannada and the placement of sound can be judged. It is not a finished film and it is not what anybody should publish, re-post or hand to a printer, a sponsor or a news desk.

What is temporary in it: the narration is a synthetic ElevenLabs read and not a human Kannada voice; there is no music at all, because no licence has been cleared; the 14 sound effects are an audition sitting under an unsigned Trust decision (07 item 5.8); and two of them are placeholder files that have to be replaced. The picture uses the consent fallback, so several photographs are stand-ins.

Creative team: everything needed to re-cut or re-grade it is in section 03, Resources. Do not work from the YouTube stream. Trustees: your notes go in section 02, Feedback.

Source: youtu.be/I6iZCH88RYU, which is 08-kannada-sfx-learning-mix.mp4: 1920x1080, 00:05:37:22, ElevenLabs narration plus the 14-cue effects pack, at -16 LUFS. Streaming it keeps the render out of this repository, so no MP4 is committed. Rebuild it locally with tools/film.py and tools/sfx.py, or the 4K equivalent with tools/master.py. Three intentional silences: cold open K01, founder quote K06 (6s, no voice), end card K46.

Captions: this cut has no burned-in subtitles. Turn on the Kannada captions with the CC button, or read them from 05-kannada-subtitles.srt. If the captions have not been attached to the upload yet, that file is what to upload as the caption track.

If the player is blank, the video visibility has been changed to Private, which blocks embedding for everyone except explicitly named accounts. Set it to Unlisted to make this page work again, or ask the owner of the upload for access.

03 · Resources

Everything the creative team needs to re-cut this

The video is a reference. These files are the source. Anyone re-cutting, re-grading, re-scoring or re-subtitling the film should work from this list and never from the YouTube stream, which is a 1080p H.264 re-compression of a re-compression.

Start with three files

tools/timeline.json is the timing source of truth: 46 shots with in and out points, durations, narration, holds, pauses, crop windows, motion, transitions, consent status and the music cue for every one. Every other document and every render is derived from it. 06 is the same thing as a plain-text EDL so the film can be rebuilt in five years without a project file. Kannada-cue-sheet.csv is the audio.

If you re-time anything, re-derive rather than re-type. Run python3 cue.py and python3 cue.py --audit-docs afterwards. The film has already moved once, from 00:05:36:01 to 00:05:37:22, and hand-typed timecodes went stale within the hour.

Rebuild the whole film

About 13 minutes on a recent laptop. Needs Python 3, ffmpeg and Playwright.

cd anniversary-video-production/kannada/tools

python3 gfx.py ../05-kannada-subtitles.srt --scale 2
python3 film.py silent.mp4
python3 stem.py <VOICE_ID>
python3 sfx.py --strict
python3 mix.py --calibrate
python3 master.py            # 4K review master
python3 master.py --check    # what still blocks a release

master.py --release is the only path to a publishable file, and it refuses to run while any of the five gates is open.

Why 4K, when no photograph is 4K

Worth being straight about this, because someone will ask.

  • The Kannada type really is 4K. All 133 graphics are re-rendered in Chrome at the target resolution, so glyphs and conjuncts are drawn sharp rather than enlarged.
  • The photography is not. The largest still in the library is 1920x1446 and 29 of 34 are under 1920px, so at 2160p they are enlarged 2x to 4x.
  • YouTube is the reason. A 2160p upload gets a better codec and a higher bitrate than a 1080p one, so even a viewer watching at 1080p sees a cleaner picture.

Judge the master on type crispness, not on photographic detail. Full measurements in 08 animatic notes, section 9.

A YouTube upload is public release. All five gates in section 04 are still open, so nothing goes up yet. Run python3 tools/master.py --check for the current list. The build script refuses to produce a release master while any gate is unwaived, and refuses outright while the narration is the scratch track.

10 · Last shot of the film

End roll and acknowledgements

The credit roll is written in both languages, but it is not buildable yet. The repository holds inconsistent spellings, near-duplicates and first-name-only entries, so the roll is gated on a trustee name check.

The rule

No name goes on screen until a trustee has confirmed its spelling and the person has agreed to be named.

This covers trustees, staff, volunteers, donors and partner organisations. Getting a name wrong in a ten-year film is the one error the audience will remember, and it cannot be fixed after the screening.

What to do

  1. Trustees work through the verification list in 13 credits and acknowledgements, section 4.
  2. Producer records each confirmed spelling and each consent to be named.
  3. Designer builds the roll only from the confirmed list, using the fonts and tokens in section 06.
  4. Tick the end-roll gate in section 04 when it is done.
