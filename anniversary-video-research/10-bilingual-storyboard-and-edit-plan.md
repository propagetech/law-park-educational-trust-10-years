# 10 · Bilingual Storyboard and Edit Plan

Two synchronised scene-by-scene plans against **Version A** of each script (the formal event cut, which is what plays in the hall).
Version B re-times the same asset order with longer holds; the deltas are noted at the end.

**Masters:** 1920x1080, 25 fps, ProRes 422 HQ. One visual master, two audio stems.
**Asset paths** are given as `assets/images/...` because those filenames encode the year and subject. Every one has a byte-identical `public/images/slide_*` twin, mapped in [04-media-inventory.csv](04-media-inventory.csv).
**Always grade from the `.jpg`, never the `.webp`.** See `05`, section 2.2.

**Consent gate.** Every shot marked `[C]` shows identifiable minors and cannot go to picture lock until a signed guardian release exists. See `14`.

## C · The six deliberate differences between the two cuts

1. **Cold open 2 s longer in Kannada.** The school signage is Kannada text the audience will actually read.
2. **All title and statistics cards get 1.3x dwell in Kannada.** Kannada glyphs carry more internal detail and need more time. See `07` section 9.
3. **The founder's quote card runs 6 s in Kannada against 5 s in English**, with no narration under it.
4. **The 2020 poster gets a Kannada subtitle strip.** The poster itself is in English; without a gloss it excludes the Kannada audience.
5. **The school-sign shot holds 1 s longer.**
6. **The Udayavani clipping holds 2 s longer in Kannada**, pushed into the caption. In English it is evidence of coverage; in Kannada it is a sentence the audience reads for themselves. This is the single moment where the Kannada version is genuinely better than the English one.

Everything else, including every asset and every cut point, is shared. This keeps conform and grading to one pass.

## F · Assembly order for the editor

1. Lay both narration stems against the asset order above. Narration first, images second.
2. Build the navy card templates once, as a single graphics project with a text layer that swaps between EN and KN. Do not build two sets of cards.
3. Grade once, on the shared visual master.
4. Conform the Kannada cut by adjusting only the six timing differences listed in section C.
5. Mix twice. Same music bed, different narration stem.
6. Export the 16:9 masters, then re-frame for 9:16 and 1:1 using the portrait-source substitutions listed in section E.

**Do not begin the 9:16 and 1:1 versions until the 16:9 Kannada cut is approved.** The Kannada text layout is the constraint that drives every other format.
