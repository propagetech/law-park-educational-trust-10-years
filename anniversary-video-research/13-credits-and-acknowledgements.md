## 3 · How to handle personal names across the two versions

**Recommendation: keep every personal name in Roman script in both language versions.**

Reasons:
1. The repository has no Kannada spelling for any donor or team member. Transliterating roughly 130 names from Roman to Kannada would introduce roughly 130 opportunities for error in names of real people, several of whom are not Kannada speakers at all (donors in the USA, Germany, Denmark, Dubai, Hong Kong, Abu Dhabi, UK).
2. Many donors will screenshot the credit roll. They will want to see the spelling they use themselves.
3. A mixed roll, Kannada headings with Roman names, is standard practice in Kannada film and television credits and reads as completely normal.

**Exception:** the two trustees' names appear in the film body as lower thirds, so they should be set in Kannada in the Kannada version, using the forms in `12` section 7, after both trustees approve them.

Organisation names follow the opposite rule: they are rendered in Kannada in the Kannada version, because they are Kannada organisations operating in Kannada, and `ಬೆಳಕು ಟ್ರಸ್ಟ್` is attested in the Udayavani clipping.

## 5 · Pacing the credit roll

- A donor roll of ~100 names at a readable scroll speed takes 45 to 60 seconds. **That is longer than a fifth of the film.**
- **Recommendation:** put a short gratitude card in the 5-minute cut (see `12` section 9), and build the full credit roll as a **separate 90-second "With Gratitude" film** for the website, the donor mailing and the event's interval or closing.
- If the roll must sit inside the main film, use three columns and a 40-second scroll, and place it after the end card so anyone leaving the hall has already seen the closing message.
- Set the roll in Quicksand 500 / Noto Sans Kannada 500 at a minimum 32 px on a 1080p master. Names below that size are unreadable on a projector and insulting to the people being thanked.
- **Alphabetise by first name**, since that is how the repository stores them and how most of these people are known to the Trust.
