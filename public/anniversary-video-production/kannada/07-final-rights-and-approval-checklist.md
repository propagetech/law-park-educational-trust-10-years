**Nothing in this list is optional. Nothing marked BLOCKING may be waived by a deadline.**

This is the Kannada cut's own checklist, scoped to the 46 shots actually in the timeline. It is narrower than [`14-rights-consent-and-publishing-checklist.md`](../../anniversary-video-research/14-rights-consent-and-publishing-checklist.md), which covers both language versions and the whole asset library; where an item here corresponds to one there, the reference is given. `14` remains the governing document.

Owner and Date columns are for the Trust to complete.

## Part 0 · The state of play, in one paragraph

The Kannada cut is editorially complete and technically buildable today. **It cannot be screened anywhere, including at the Trust's own event, until guardian consent exists for the children in 22 of its 46 shots**, Udayavani grants permission for the newspaper clipping, and a music licence is in place. Nineteen shots carrying 2 minutes 12 seconds are already clear, which means a colder fallback cut exists if consent runs late (`14` Part 12). The consent process is the longest lead-time item in the project by a wide margin and it should start before the edit, not after it.

## Part 12 · If the deadline arrives before consent does

**Do not screen the film with unconsented children in it.** A film taken down after the event because a parent objected does more harm to the Trust's standing, and to that family, than a colder film screened on time.

This cut is already structured for that fallback. **Nineteen shots are clear today**, carrying 2 minutes 12 seconds: `K01` `K04` `K05` `K06` `K07` `K10` `K12` `K17` `K18` `K22` `K25` `K26` `K27` `K29` `K31` `K34` `K35` `K37` `K46`. Nine typographic cards, four school signage and building frames with no person in them, three face-free interiors and objects, two documents, and one consent-driven crop.

To reach five minutes without a single unconsented child:
