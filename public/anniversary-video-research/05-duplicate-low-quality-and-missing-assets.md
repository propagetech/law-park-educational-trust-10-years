## 2. Duplicate structure

### 2.1 Systematic, by design
`assets/images/` is a renamed mirror of `public/`. `assets/images/manifest.json` records 243 mappings, and `assets/images/README.md` states the originals were deliberately left in place. Every one of the 114 `slide_XX_Picture_Y_ZZ` files in `public/images/` has a byte-identical twin in `assets/images/timeline/` under a descriptive name.

**Production instruction: source every shot from `assets/images/timeline/` and the other `assets/images/` subfolders, never from `public/images/slide_*`.** The `assets` names encode the year and the subject, which makes the edit decision list readable and auditable. The mapping is in the `duplicate_status` column of `04-media-inventory.csv`.

Example: `public/images/slide_19_Picture_2_01.webp` is byte-identical to `assets/images/timeline/2024-children-holding-school-bags.webp`.

### 2.2 Format pairs
Almost every photograph exists as both `.jpg`/`.jpeg` and `.webp`. **Always grade from the JPEG**, never the WebP: the WebP files were generated by `scripts/optimize-images.mjs` for web delivery and carry compression artefacts that become visible on a projected screen and under a Ken Burns push.

### 2.3 Genuine near-duplicates within the same shoot
These are separate frames of the same moment. Pick one per sequence, do not cut between them:

| Group | Files | Pick |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 car-boot supplies | `2024-car-trunk-filled-with-books-and-supplies.jpg`, `2024-car-trunk-filled-with-school-bags.jpg` | The books version, it reads better as a detail insert |
| 2025 supplies handover | `2025-children-reaching-for-supplies.jpg`, `group-reaching-hands-for-supplies.jpeg`, `hands-reaching-for-school-supplies.jpeg` | `hands-reaching-for-school-supplies.jpeg` for the tight hands detail |

## 3. Resolution and technical quality

### 3.1 The 960x540 problem
Roughly a third of the timeline library was extracted from a PowerPoint deck (the `slide_XX_Picture` naming and the `PROJECT_SUMMARY.md` note about `slides_data.json`). Many of those frames are **960x540**, which is below 1080p.

**Consequence for the edit:** a 960x540 image cannot fill a 1920x1080 frame without visible softening, and cannot be used at all on a 4K master. Options, in order of preference:
1. Ask the trustees for the camera originals. Most were taken on phones and will exist at 3000 px or more.
2. Use them at less than full frame: inside a photo-card treatment, a split screen, or a timeline collage where the image sits at 40 to 60 percent of frame width.
3. Use them as a soft, heavily blurred background layer behind a title card.

Do **not** AI-upscale documentary photographs of real children and then present the result as archival record.

### 3.3 Logo problem
The only logo files are `public/images/logo.png` and `logo-copy.png` (artwork on a **solid black background**), `logo-purple.png` (on a solid purple background), and `public/logo.webp`. **There is no transparent-background PNG and no vector logo anywhere in the repository.**

A hard black or purple rectangle around the logo will look like an error in a title card and in the end card. This is the single most important missing asset. See section 6.
