# Kannada music map

The score, as a brief a composer can work from. One bed serves both language
versions; only the narration stem changes. `07` section 11.

**Film:** `00:05:57:22` = 357.88 s, 25 fps. **There is no music in this
repository.** `07` item 5.1, the licence gate, is BLOCKING and unsigned, and
nothing below exists yet.

---

## 1 · The one-paragraph brief

Five minutes of sparse acoustic score with a Karnataka tonal centre and no
rhythmic drive. A solo bansuri carries a four-note motif that returns eight
times, each time in a different register or instrument. A felt piano and a low
cello enter behind it. A near-static drone underneath holds the film together
through three complete music-outs. Soft frame drum appears exactly once, for
17.9 seconds, and never returns. Nothing climaxes. The film resolves rather than
arrives.

**If a supplier cannot deliver three things, the bed is wrong regardless of how
good the melody is:** six seconds of complete silence at `K06`, the drop-out
under ಪೂರ್ತಿ ಅಲ್ಲ. at `K27`, and the refusal to climax at `K43`.

---

## 2 · Palette

| Instrument | Role | Enters | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bansuri** | The film's voice. Carries the motif | `K01`, 00:00:00:00 | Single-note phrases and short motifs. Breath is welcome. Not devotional, not television-serial phrasing |
| **Low drone** | Continuity through every silence and every drop | `K01` | Tanpura-like or a warm synthetic pad. Near-static. Minimal and modern, not devotional. This is what returns first after each music-out |
| **Felt piano** | The origin story and, later, gratitude | `K08`, 00:00:45:24 | Open voicings, sparse notes, gentle broken patterns. Never repetitive inspirational-corporate piano |
| **Cello** | Weight under the founders and the method | `K08` | Long held notes. One low note is usually enough |
| **Soft violin ensemble** | A bed, never a lead | `K19`, 00:02:08:04 | Slow swells and gentle counter-melodies. No aggressive orchestral strings and no Hollywood victory chord |
| **Soft frame drum** | Forward movement, once | `K22`, 00:02:28:12 | Very light, muted, no groove. Out by `K24`. Never returns. No fast rhythm, no dance rhythm, no claps, no motivational beat |
| **Nylon guitar** | Alternative to the piano at gratitude | `K38`, 00:04:42:06 | Either the piano or the guitar takes `K38`, not both |

**Not in the palette at all:** vocals in any language, brass, cymbals, timpani,
orchestral tutti, trailer impacts, EDM risers, synthesised plucks, harp glissandi,
devotional cadence, four-to-the-floor percussion.

## 3 · The motif

Four notes, stated bare by the bansuri at `K02` over the first narration line,
then developed once per act. It is the only melodic material in the film that
repeats, and it is how a five-minute score with almost no rhythm stays coherent.

| Statement | Shot | Timecode | Treatment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `K02` | 00:00:07:00 | Bansuri alone, unaccompanied, no answer |
| 2 | `K03` | 00:00:15:16 | Stops mid-phrase. Drone alone under ಬುದ್ಧಿ ಇದೆ. ಕಲಿಯುವ ಹಂಬಲ ಇದೆ. The unfinished phrase is the question the film opens with |
| 3 | `K07` | 00:00:39:00 | Returns after the six seconds of silence, warmer register. Completes this time |
| 4 | `K09` | 00:00:56:03 | Piano answers it once, an octave down |
| 5 | `K14` | 00:01:26:00 | Bansuri and piano together. The first time it has company |
| 6 | `K15` | 00:01:32:02 | One small variation per year across the timeline. Cello counter-line |
| 7 | `K32` | 00:03:45:13 | Piano takes it, bansuri answers. Roles swapped |
| 8 | `K40` | 00:05:06:12 | Piano, quietly, under the parents. The last complete statement |
| 9 | `K41` to `K44` | 00:05:09:07 | Opens out, piano and bansuri together, and **resolves without a climax** |

## 4 · Intensity curve

A 1 to 5 scale. **This film never reaches 5.** `06` section 7 refuses a climax
at `K43`, so nothing before it may claim one either, and a bed that peaks at 5
anywhere has already broken the ending.

```
act 1  K01 K02 K03 K04 K05 K06                                      1 2 1 1 2 0
act 2  K07 K08 K09 K10 K11 K12 K13                                  2 2 2 1 2 0 1
act 3  K14 K15 K16 K17 K18 K19 K20 K21 K22 K23 K24 K25              3 3 1 1 3 3 3 3 4 4 2 2
act 4  K26 K27 K28 K29 K30 K31 K32 K33 K34 K35 K36 K37 K38 K39 K40  2 0 1 2 2 3 3 3 1 3 3 3 3 3 3
act 5  K41 K42 K43 K44 K45 K46                                      4 3 4 3 2 2
```

| Level | Reads as | Seconds | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Nothing. Room tone only | 18.6s | `K06`, `K12`, `K27` |
| **1** | Drone, or one held note | 67.1s | `K01`, `K03`, `K04`, `K10`, `K13`, `K16`, `K17`, `K28`, `K34` |
| **2** | Drone plus one instrument, sparse | | `K02`, `K05`, `K07` to `K09`, `K11`, `K24` to `K26`, `K29`, `K30`, `K45`, `K46` |
| **3** | Motif with company. The film's working level | | most of acts 3 and 4 |
| **4** | Open. Two or three instruments, still no percussion after `K24` | | `K22`, `K23`, `K41`, `K43` |
| **5** | Never used | 0s | Reserved and refused |

**85.7 seconds of the film, a quarter of it, sits at intensity 1 or lower.**
That is the design, not an omission.

## 5 · Act by act

Per-shot cues, levels and fades are in
[`Kannada-cue-sheet.csv`](Kannada-cue-sheet.csv). This is the shape.

### Act 1 · ಆರಂಭ, the child and the question
`00:00:00:00` to `00:00:36:10`, 36.4 s, intensity 0 to 2

Begin almost in silence. Drone at -30 dB rising to -24 over four seconds, and a
single bansuri breath before any phrase. The motif states itself once at `K02`,
then **stops mid-phrase** at `K03`. Melody out entirely at `K04` so the
school-fee line lands on the drone. A fifth under the title at `K05`, resolving
to one held note. Then `K06`: **out completely, six seconds, room tone only.**

Do not put music under the opening Kannada narration at any level that competes
with it. Silence is part of the opening: 16.5 of these 36.4 seconds are written
holds and pauses.

### Act 2 · 2016, one beginning
`00:00:36:10` to `00:01:19:17`, 43.2 s, intensity 0 to 2

Reduce the score. Bansuri returns at `K07` and completes the motif for the first
time. Felt piano and one low cello note enter under the founders at `K08` and
`K09`. Thin to drone across the 2 s pause at `K10`. Piano alone, three notes, at
`K11`. Then `K12`: **silence** under ಒಂದು ಮಗು. ಒಂದು ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿವೇತನ., and 1.0 s
of hold after the line before the cut. Drone alone returns at -30 dB at `K13`.

The temptation here is a warm swell under the founder's story. Resist it. The
whole act is at intensity 2 or below.

### Act 3 · ಒಂದು ದಶಕದ ಹಾದಿ, ten years of showing up
`00:01:19:17` to `00:02:44:13`, 84.9 s, intensity 1 to 4

The only act that builds. Motif enters properly at `K14` with piano. One small
variation per year through `K15`. **Then it falls away**: a single held note at
`K16` for 2020, drone only at `K17` through the relief poster. Melody returns
warmer at `K18` for the libraries, strings join at `K19`.

Soft frame drum enters at `K22`, for the first and only time in the film, and
the build to `K23` is the highest point in the score at -20 dB. **No swell on
the numbers.** `K23` lands warm under ಮುನ್ನೂರು and then holds through 2.5
seconds of silence without filling it. Percussion out at `K24`, down to a
single line.

### Act 4 · ಕೆಲಸದ ಕ್ರಮ, method, care and community
`00:02:44:13` to `00:04:51:19`, 127.2 s, intensity 0 to 3

The longest act and the least musical. Rhythmic movement gone by `K26`: warm
cello, quiet piano, soft drone.

`K27` is **music out completely** under ಪೂರ್ತಿ ಅಲ್ಲ., with the drone alone
returning at -30 dB on the 2 s pause. `K29` takes **one low tonal emphasis**,
a cello or bansuri tonic, under ಬೇರೆ ಯಾರ ಕೈಗೂ ಅಲ್ಲ. That is a note in the
score, not a sound effect, and it is the only low emphasis in the act.

Tempo lifts slightly at `K31` for the programmes. `K34` is the film's most
careful cue: **drone and one held bansuri note at -28 dB, nothing that could be
read as sentiment**, across 12.8 seconds naming single-parent households, HIV
in families and long illness, over a card with no photograph. Warmth returns at
`K35` for the partners and holds without swelling through the newspaper coverage
at `K36`.

`K37`, the award: **one gentle lift, then settle. No fanfare.** No brass, no
cymbal, no chime, no applause. Piano or nylon guitar enters for the first time
at `K38` and the gratitude section runs warm and level to the end of the act.

### Act 5 · ಮುಂದಿನ ದಾರಿ, future and anniversary welcome
`00:04:51:19` to `00:05:57:22`, 46.1 s, intensity 2 to 4

Open up at `K41`: piano and bansuri together at -20 dB, with a low pulse that
reads as forward movement rather than rhythm. One rising interval at `K42`, not
a modulation. `K43` **lifts but does not climax**: no added percussion, no
cymbal, no key change, no octave doubling. Resolve unhurried at `K44`.

`K45` steps down 2 dB under the welcome line and adds no new material, so the
narrator stays central through:

```
ಲಾ ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಎಜುಕೇಷನಲ್ ಟ್ರಸ್ಟ್‌ನ ಹತ್ತನೇ ವರ್ಷದ
ಸಂಭ್ರಮಕ್ಕೆ ನಿಮಗೆಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಆತ್ಮೀಯ ಸ್ವಾಗತ.
```

The voice finishes at `00:05:30:22`. Then the end card: a final warm chord, one
gentle chime at `00:05:32:22`, and a natural fade to silence over the last
3 seconds. No boom. No hard cut after the logo.

## 6 · What a composer or supplier must deliver

| # | Item | Spec |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Full bed, 357.88 s | 48 kHz, 24-bit WAV, one file, starting at 00:00:00:00 with no head trim. Silence written into the file at `K06`, `K12` and `K27`, not left to the mixer |
| 2 | Stems | Bansuri, piano and guitar, strings and cello, drone, percussion. Same length, same start |
| 3 | Levels | Mixed to sit 12 to 18 dB below narration. `tools/mix.py --music` will conform the bed and measure the result |
| 4 | Licence | Public event screening, YouTube monetised or not, social media and website embedding, worldwide, in perpetuity. `07` items 5.1 and 5.2. A personal-use or single-project non-broadcast licence is not sufficient |
| 5 | Paperwork | Licence certificate, track title, composer and licence number, recorded and credited. If commissioned, a written assignment or licence from the musician. `07` items 5.2 and 5.3 |
| 6 | No vocals | In any language. `14` item 5.4 |

**Commissioning five minutes of original bansuri from a Karnataka musician is
cheaper than premium library licensing and is a better story.** `07` section 11
says so, `06` section 7 repeats it, and it also solves items 4, 5 and 6 in one
transaction. A library search that satisfies all six is likely to be slower and
more expensive than a phone call.
