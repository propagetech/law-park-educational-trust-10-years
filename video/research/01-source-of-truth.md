## 2. Mission and philosophy

**Core mission [V, MIS-01]:** LPET helps children from rural areas across India get their right to education through funded scholarships.

**Founder's line, used consistently across the site and magazine [V, MIS-02]:**
> "When it comes to education, no child deserves to be left behind."
> Charulatha M. R., Founder

**Operating belief on partial funding [V, MIS-03]:** LPET pays up to 75% of tuition. Parents cover the remainder so that they stay involved. From `websiteContent.ts`: "We believe that anything given for fully free will have no value."

**Anti-leakage principle [V, MIS-04]:** Fees are paid directly to the school, never to parents, "to prevent misappropriation of the funds."

**Who LPET reaches [V, MIS-05]:** children in the rural sector, tribal children, children of HIV-affected families, children with chronic health conditions, children of single parents.
Additional category "children of acid-attack victims" appears only in invitation letters [C, MIS-06].

## 3. The four-step method

Verbatim from [data/websiteContent.ts](../../website/data/websiteContent.ts) `process`. All four [V].

| Step | Claim | Substance |
|---|---|---|
| 1. **Identify** | PRC-01 | School principals, well-wishers and citizens nominate deserving students from income-deprived backgrounds. |
| 2. **Validate** | PRC-02 | The team travels to the location, gathers students with their parents in one place, and interviews each student and parent to verify the case. |
| 3. **Embrace** | PRC-03 | LPET pays up to 75% of tuition fees at the child's current school; parents cover the rest. |
| 4. **Incubate** | PRC-04 | Fees go directly to the school so children can focus on studies. |

## 6. People

### Trustees [V]

**Charulatha M. R.**, Founder and Managing Trustee (PPL-01).
From `websiteContent.ts`: taught poor children in her locality free of cost during her young age, which became the foundation for the Trust. B.A. Economics, Chennai; LL.B., Bengaluru; PGDIPRL, National Law School of India University, Bengaluru. Paid fees for children even before the Trust existed. "Most of the times she is surrounded with children asking doubts in their studies which continued even today."

**S. M. Manjunatha**, Trustee (PPL-02).
From Sadenahalli, a village in Karnataka. First member of his family to leave the village to study in the city. Law graduate, Bengaluru. Funded many children from his village who are now well-settled.

**[C, PPL-03]** A donor letter states Charulatha and Manjunatha are married ("Along with my husband Mr. Manjunatha S. M we started this Trust"). This is a single source and a personal detail. Do not put it on screen without explicit trustee permission.
