# 09 · ಕನ್ನಡ ಚಿತ್ರಕಥೆ · Kannada Five-Minute Script

ಲಾ ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಎಜುಕೇಷನಲ್ ಟ್ರಸ್ಟ್ · ಹತ್ತು ವರ್ಷಗಳ ಸಂಭ್ರಮ · ಸ್ವಾಗತ ಚಿತ್ರ

This is **not a translation of `08`.** It is an independent Kannada script carrying the same verified facts, written to be spoken naturally on a stage in Karnataka. Sentence order, imagery and idiom differ from the English wherever a literal rendering would sound translated. The adaptation decisions are documented in section 3.

**ಆಧಾರ / Evidence:** claim IDs in square brackets resolve in [02-evidence-table.csv](02-evidence-table.csv).
**ಟ್ರಸ್ಟಿ ದೃಢೀಕರಣ:** lines needing approval are marked **[ಟ್ರಸ್ಟಿ ದೃಢೀಕರಣ ಅಗತ್ಯ]**.

**ಸಂಕೇತಗಳು / Conventions**
- `ಧ್ವನಿ:` spoken narration (voice-over)
- `ಪರದೆ ಪಠ್ಯ:` onscreen text, not spoken
- `[ವಿರಾಮ 2ಸೆ]` deliberate pause, 2 seconds

**ಸೂಚನೆ:** ಈ ಚಿತ್ರಕಥೆಯಲ್ಲಿ ಮಕ್ಕಳ ಒಟ್ಟು ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ, ಹಳ್ಳಿಗಳ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ ಅಥವಾ ಜಿಲ್ಲೆಗಳ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆಯನ್ನು ಎಲ್ಲಿಯೂ ಹೇಳಿಲ್ಲ. ದಾಖಲೆಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಆ ಅಂಕಿಗಳು ಪರಸ್ಪರ ಹೊಂದಿಕೆಯಾಗುತ್ತಿಲ್ಲ. ನೋಡಿ `06`.
*(No cumulative totals appear in this script. Those figures conflict across the repository. See `06`.)*

# ಆವೃತ್ತಿ ಎ · ವೇದಿಕೆಯ ಔಪಚಾರಿಕ ಚಿತ್ರಕಥೆ
# VERSION A · Formal anniversary-event script

**ಪದಗಳ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ (ಧ್ವನಿ ಮಾತ್ರ): 471 ಕನ್ನಡ ಪದಗಳು**
**ಅಂದಾಜು ಅವಧಿ: 5 ನಿಮಿಷ 00 ಸೆಕೆಂಡು** = 471 ಪದ, ನಿಮಿಷಕ್ಕೆ 100 ಪದಗಳಂತೆ (4 ನಿ 43 ಸೆ) + 17 ಸೆ ವಿರಾಮ
*(ನಿಮಿಷಕ್ಕೆ 105 ಪದಗಳ ವೇಗದಲ್ಲಿ ಓದಿದರೆ 4 ನಿ 46 ಸೆ. ಇಂಗ್ಲಿಷ್ ಆವೃತ್ತಿಗೆ ಹೊಂದಿಸಲು ನಿಧಾನವಾಗಿ ಓದಿ.)*
**ಧಾಟಿ:** ಗೌರವಪೂರ್ಣ, ಸಮಾಧಾನದ ಮಾತು. ಸಭೆಯನ್ನು ಉದ್ದೇಶಿಸಿ ನಿಂತು ಓದುವ ಶೈಲಿ.

> **Timing note.** Formal Kannada narration runs at roughly 100 to 110 words per minute, against 140 for English. This script is therefore about 30 percent shorter in word count than `08` for the same running time. That is expected and correct. **Do not add material to match the English word count.**
>
> | | English `08` | Kannada `09` |
> |---|---|---|
> | Version A | 669 words + 15 s pause = **5:01** | 471 words + 17 s pause = **5:00** |
> | Version B | 664 words + 31 s pause = **5:15** | 440 words + 36 s pause = **5:00** |
>
> Kannada Version B lands 15 seconds shorter than English Version B. Do not pad the narration. The 15 seconds are absorbed by longer dwell on the Kannada title cards, which they need anyway. See `10`, Kannada edit plan.

# 3 · English-to-Kannada Adaptation Notes

Where the Kannada deliberately departs from the English, and why. Read this before any review, so that a reviewer does not "correct" the Kannada back towards a literal translation.

### 3.1 Structural adaptations

| # | English | Kannada | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "one Trust has stood at exactly that point" | "ಒಂದು ಟ್ರಸ್ಟ್ ಸರಿಯಾಗಿ ಅಲ್ಲಿಯೇ **ಬಂದು ನಿಂತಿದೆ**" | "Stood at" is an English spatial metaphor. ಬಂದು ನಿಂತಿದೆ, "has come and stood", carries the sense of someone arriving and staying, which is what the Trust actually does. |
| 2 | "a school fee decides whether they keep going" | "ಶಾಲಾ ಶುಲ್ಕ ಒಂದು ತೀರ್ಮಾನ **ತೆಗೆದುಕೊಂಡು ಬಿಡುತ್ತದೆ**" | The ಬಿಡು auxiliary adds finality and a note of regret that the plain verb lacks. This is the single most important verb choice in the opening. |
| 3 | "the map widened" | "**ವ್ಯಾಪ್ತಿ ಹಿಗ್ಗಿತು**" | A literal ನಕ್ಷೆ ಅಗಲವಾಯಿತು would be meaningless. ವ್ಯಾಪ್ತಿ (scope, reach) is the natural Kannada abstraction. |
| 4 | "the shelves went up" | "ಪುಸ್ತಕದ **ಕಪಾಟುಗಳು ಎದ್ದವು**" | Kept the physical image rather than saying "libraries were established". ಎದ್ದವು, "rose", is idiomatic for construction and keeps the concrete register the English aims for. |
| 5 | "at the age when a decision quietly gets made" | "ಬದುಕಿನ ದಾರಿ **ಸದ್ದಿಲ್ಲದೆ** ನಿರ್ಧಾರವಾಗುವ ವಯಸ್ಸು ಅದು" | Added ಬದುಕಿನ ದಾರಿ (life's road) because Kannada needs the object made explicit; the English can leave it implied. |
| 6 | "a share that a parent carries is a share that a parent protects" | "ತಾವೂ ಒಂದು ಪಾಲು ಹೊತ್ತ ಪೋಷಕರು ಮಗುವಿನ **ಓದಿನ ಒಳಗೇ ಉಳಿಯುತ್ತಾರೆ**" | The English wordplay on carry/protect does not exist in Kannada. Replaced with a stronger and more concrete idea: the parent "stays inside the child's studies". |
| 7 | "There was no launch." | "ಯಾವ **ಉದ್ಘಾಟನೆಯೂ** ಇರಲಿಲ್ಲ." | ಉದ್ಘಾಟನೆ (inauguration) is exactly the right cultural referent: the ribbon, the chief guest, the photograph. Sharper in Kannada than "launch" is in English. |
| 8 | "Not a form. A conversation." | "**ಅರ್ಜಿ ಅಲ್ಲ. ಮಾತುಕತೆ.**" | Kept as short as the English. ಅರ್ಜಿ carries the full weight of Indian bureaucratic paperwork. |
| 9 | "the years that do not make a good photograph" | "**ಒಳ್ಳೆಯ ಫೋಟೋ ಸಿಗದ ವರ್ಷಗಳು**" | Retained the English word ಫೋಟೋ, which is what everyone in Karnataka actually says. ಛಾಯಾಚಿತ್ರ would sound like a government notice. |
| 10 | "who have never met the children they paid for" | "ತಾವು ಶುಲ್ಕ ಕಟ್ಟಿದ ಮಕ್ಕಳನ್ನು ಎಂದೂ ನೋಡದ ..." | Restructured into a Kannada relative clause preceding the noun, which is the natural order. A post-modifying clause would sound translated. |
| 11 | "One promise, kept quietly" | "**ಸದ್ದಿಲ್ಲದೆ ಉಳಿಸಿಕೊಂಡ ಒಂದು ಮಾತು**" | ಮಾತು, "a word given", is warmer and more Kannada than ಭರವಸೆ or ವಾಗ್ದಾನ, which sound legal. ಉಳಿಸಿಕೊಳ್ಳು is the natural verb for keeping one's word. |
| 12 | "Welcome to the tenth anniversary" | "ಹತ್ತನೇ ವರ್ಷದ **ಸಂಭ್ರಮಕ್ಕೆ** ನಿಮಗೆಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಆತ್ಮೀಯ ಸ್ವಾಗತ" | Added ಸಂಭ್ರಮ (joyous occasion) and ಆತ್ಮೀಯ (warm, close) because a bare ಸ್ವಾಗತ is too flat to close a film on. This is the standard warm register for a Kannada stage welcome. |

### 3.3 Terminology fixed for the whole production

Full glossary in `12`. The four decisions that most affect the sound of the film:

1. **ಲಾ ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಎಜುಕೇಷನಲ್ ಟ್ರಸ್ಟ್**, exactly as printed in Udayavani on 19 June 2024 `[ORG-02]`. The name is never translated.

### 3.4 Numbers

Spoken as Kannada number words (ನೂರೈವತ್ತು, ಇನ್ನೂರು, ಮುನ್ನೂರು, ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತು, ಎಪ್ಪತ್ತೈದು) because a narrator reading Arabic numerals in Kannada narration produces an inconsistent register. Onscreen text uses Arabic numerals (150, 200, 300, 20, 75%), which is standard practice in Kannada print and signage.

Years are spoken as "ಎರಡು ಸಾವಿರದ ಹದಿನಾರು" style or as the English digits, at the narrator's comfort. Record both and choose in the edit.

**The one line an editor should be able to recognise by ear**, because it is the film's closing beat and every cut hangs off it:

> ಲಾ ಪಾರ್ಕ್ ಎಜುಕೇಷನಲ್ ಟ್ರಸ್ಟ್‌ನ ಹತ್ತನೇ ವರ್ಷದ ಸಂಭ್ರಮಕ್ಕೆ ನಿಮಗೆಲ್ಲರಿಗೂ ಆತ್ಮೀಯ ಸ್ವಾಗತ.
> *lah park e-ju-KAY-shun-al TRUST-na hat-ta-nay VAR-sha-da sam-BHRA-ma-kke ni-ma-gel-la-ri-goo aat-MEE-ya SWAA-ga-ta*
> "A warm welcome to you all, to the tenth-year celebration of Law Park Educational Trust."
