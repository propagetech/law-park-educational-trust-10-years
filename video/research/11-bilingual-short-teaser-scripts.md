## 5 · Social captions, English

**Instagram and Facebook, teaser post**

> In 2016, two people drove from Bengaluru to Chickaballapur and gave one child a scholarship. On the same visit, 150 students received a steel plate and a steel glass.
>
> Ten years on, the method has not changed. Someone puts forward a child's name. The team drives out. They sit with the family. And Law Park Educational Trust pays up to 75% of the school fee, directly to the school.
>
> Libraries in rural schools. Career guidance for 9th and 10th standard students. 200 school bags at MM Hills in 2024. 300 in H.D. Kote in 2025.
>
> Ten years. One child at a time.
>
> Watch the full film: journey.lawparkeducationaltrust.org
>
> #LawParkEducationalTrust #10Years #RuralEducation #Karnataka #EducationForAll #Scholarships

**LinkedIn, for donors and CSR partners**

> Law Park Educational Trust completes ten years in 2026.
>
> The model is deliberately partial. The Trust covers up to 75% of a child's school fee and the family covers the rest, because a parent who carries part of the cost stays involved in their child's education. Fees are paid directly to the school, never through an intermediary.
>
> Every child is identified through a nomination, and every nomination is verified in person, with the child and the parents present.
>
> Ten years of that method: scholarships from Chickaballapur to H.D. Kote, libraries in rural schools, career guidance for students choosing their stream, and 500 school bags distributed across 2024 and 2025.
>
> In December 2025 the Founder, Charulatha M. R., received the Bharat Shiksha Ratan Award from the Economic and Social Development Foundation at the National Summit in New Delhi.
>
> journey.lawparkeducationaltrust.org

*(Note: "500 school bags across 2024 and 2025" is the sum of two verified figures, 200 and 300. It is the only aggregate anywhere in this package, and it is arithmetic on verified numbers rather than an unverified claim.)*
