# 12 · Bilingual Onscreen Text and Lower Thirds

Every piece of text that appears on screen, in English and in natural Kannada.
Kannada is not a gloss of the English. Where the natural Kannada differs in structure or emphasis, a literal back-translation is given in italics so a non-Kannada reviewer can check the meaning.

**Type spec** (from `07` section 9)
- Display and year numerals: Playfair Display 700-900 / **Noto Serif Kannada 700**
- Body, lower thirds, labels: Quicksand 500-600 / **Noto Sans Kannada 500-600**
- Kannada at **1.35x** the English point size, line height 1.5, **no letterspacing**
- Colours: white or gold-light `#e0b06a` on navy `#1c1c2e`. Never gold `#c9903e` for text.

## 6 · The four-step method

| English | Kannada | Back-translation |
|---|---|---|
| **Identify** / Someone puts forward a child's name | **ಗುರುತಿಸುವುದು** / ಯಾರೋ ಒಂದು ಮಗುವಿನ ಹೆಸರು ಸೂಚಿಸುತ್ತಾರೆ | *Identifying / Someone suggests a child's name* |
| **Validate** / We meet every child and every parent | **ಪರಿಶೀಲಿಸುವುದು** / ಪ್ರತಿ ಮಗು ಮತ್ತು ಪ್ರತಿ ಪೋಷಕರನ್ನೂ ಭೇಟಿಯಾಗುತ್ತೇವೆ | *Verifying / We meet every child and every parent* |
| **Embrace** / Up to 75% of the fee. The family carries the rest. | **ಒಳಗೊಳ್ಳುವುದು** / ಶುಲ್ಕದ ಶೇಕಡ 75ರವರೆಗೆ. ಉಳಿದದ್ದು ಕುಟುಂಬದ ಪಾಲು. | *Including / Up to 75% of the fee. The rest is the family's share.* |
| **Incubate** / Paid directly to the school. Never through anyone else. | **ಬೆಳೆಸುವುದು** / ನೇರವಾಗಿ ಶಾಲೆಗೆ. ಬೇರೆ ಯಾರ ಕೈಗೂ ಅಲ್ಲ. | *Nurturing / Directly to the school. Not into anyone else's hands.* |

"Embrace" and "Incubate" have no clean Kannada equivalents in this sense. **ಒಳಗೊಳ್ಳುವುದು** ("taking in, including") and **ಬೆಳೆಸುವುದು** ("nurturing, raising") carry the intended meaning without the odd metaphor the English inherits. Note that the Kannada set is grammatically consistent, all four are verbal nouns, which the English set is not.
