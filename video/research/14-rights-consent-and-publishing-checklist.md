**Nothing in this package may be published until every item marked BLOCKING is signed off.**

## Part 1 · The one thing that matters most

Every photograph in this film shows identifiable children. Many were taken at tribal schools, at scholarship interviews with families present, and at support sessions for children from HIV-affected households.

**Law Park Educational Trust holds no consent or release records inside this repository.** There is not a single consent form, release, or permission note anywhere in `public/`, `assets/`, or the data files.

That is not a criticism of the Trust. Photographs taken over ten years for a website and a magazine were almost certainly taken with the knowledge and goodwill of the schools and families. But a five-minute film that will be projected at an event, posted to YouTube, shared on WhatsApp, and reposted by donors is a materially different act of publication, and it needs a materially stronger basis.

**Start the consent process before the edit, not after it.** It is the longest lead-time item in the project by a wide margin.

## Part 12 · If a deadline forces a compromise

If consent cannot be completed in time, **do not publish the film with unconsented children in it.** There is a workable fallback:

1. Cut a version that uses only: the school exteriors with no people, the supplies and stationery details, the empty library shelves, the car boot, the two posters, the newspaper clipping, the award certificate, the trustee portraits, and typographic cards.
2. That version can carry the entire narration. The `assets/images/timeline/` folder contains enough object, building and document photography to sustain five minutes: `2025-government-primary-school-sign.jpg`, `2025-colorful-school-building.jpg`, `2025-saraswati-primary-school-sign.jpg`, `2022-library-bookshelves.jpg`, `2024-car-trunk-filled-with-books-and-supplies.jpg`, `2024-snack-packets-for-distribution.jpg`, `2025-stationery-and-snacks-arranged.jpg`, `2025-stacked-school-bags-close-up.jpg`, `2024-school-supply-kit-on-floor.jpg`, plus both posters, the clipping and the certificate.
3. It will be a colder film. It will also be a publishable one, and it can be reissued with the children's photography once consent is complete.

A film that has to be taken down after the event because a parent objects does more harm to the Trust's standing, and to that family, than a slightly less warm film screened on time.
